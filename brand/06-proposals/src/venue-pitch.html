What we need from you

  • Floor space for four to six demo tables, ideally away from the main walkway.
  • Access to power — standard outlets are fine, we bring our own extension leads.
  • Somewhere reasonably lit to stand and talk. Headsets work in the dark; people do not.
  • A point of contact for the night.

What you get

  • A full room on a night that is usually quiet, with a crowd that stays and drinks.
  • Your venue named on the event page and every announcement, reaching our list directly.
  • Volunteers who set up, run the floor, and clear everything at the end of the night.
  • Insurance and equipment are ours. Nothing gets attached to your walls.
Happy to do a trial. Give us one night. If the room is not busier than a normal Wednesday, we will not ask again.
