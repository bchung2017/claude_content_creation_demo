What XR Guild is

XR Guild runs free, in-person demo nights for people who are curious about extended reality. Enthusiasts, educators, and complete beginners come to try headsets and show each other what they have been building. There is no membership and no ticket price.

Events are held in bars and clubs. Venues host us at no charge because a demo night fills a quiet weeknight with paying customers.
