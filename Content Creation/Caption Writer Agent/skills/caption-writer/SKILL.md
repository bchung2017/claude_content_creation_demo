# Caption writer

Read `brand.json`, `research.json`, `hook.json`, and the completed
`carousel.json`. Write a caption that:

- explains the carousel without repeating every slide;
- cites or names the relevant sources when the project requires it;
- keeps company-reported, disputed, and unverified claims visibly bounded;
- preserves important dates, conditions, and limitations;
- accurately reflects the slide sequence and CTA that were actually rendered;
- ends with a neutral reader action only when the brief requests one.

Record referenced claim ids, source ids, attribution, disclosure, and any
remaining gap in `caption.json`. Do not add account names, hashtags, growth
tactics, or a writing voice beyond the completed `brand.json`. Use the supplied
CTA style when present, but never let it overstate the evidence.
