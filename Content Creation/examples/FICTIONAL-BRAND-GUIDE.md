# Signal Garden — fictional worked example

By TheVibeFounder

This is a fictional teaching example. It is not the creator’s live brand guide
and should not be copied as a student’s identity.

## Brand name

Signal Garden

## Audience

Independent creators who want calm, practical systems for turning scattered
ideas into consistent educational content.

## Voice

- Clear
- Curious
- Grounded

Write like this: “Here is the smallest useful way to test the idea.”

Do not sound like this: “This secret will instantly change everything.”

## Colors

- Primary: Leaf green `#46B37B`
- Background: Deep soil `#14201A`
- Text: Soft white `#F4F6F2`
- Accent: Sunlight yellow `#F2C94C`

## Typography

- Headline: Arial Bold, with sans-serif fallback
- Body: Arial Regular, with sans-serif fallback
- Labels or numbers: Courier New Bold, with monospace fallback

These system fonts keep the example redistributable.

## Logo rules

Use the words “Signal Garden” in the bottom-left footer. Keep clear space equal
to the height of the capital S. Do not place it over a busy image. This demo
does not include or require a logo file.

## Imagery

- Use: simple diagrams, owned screenshots, paper textures, and close crops
- Treatment: high contrast, one focal point, short source labels
- Avoid: generic office stock photos, glossy 3D icons, and decorative clutter

## Visual direction

Quiet editorial layouts with generous spacing, one strong idea per slide, and
small garden-inspired accents used as navigation rather than decoration.

## Content rules

Always:

- Teach one practical action.
- Separate evidence from opinion.

Never:

- Promise guaranteed results.
- Hide the source of a factual claim.

## CTA style

Invite a small experiment, not a purchase. Example: “Try this on one post and
keep the part that saves you time.”
