# Social media profile

## Inspect

- Exact topic, platform, date window, region, language, and account scope.
- Original post, parent context, replies, quotes, reposts, and linked sources.
- Author identity and relationship to the subject.
- Firsthand observation versus interpretation, promotion, or repetition.
- Timestamps, visible edits, stable URLs, and missing/deleted context.
- Meaningful agreement, disagreement, counter-signals, and coverage gaps.
- The chosen sample, time window, claim-ranking method, and stopping rule.
- Unique-author and qualifying-post counts for each required platform,
  including explicit zeros.

## Evidence boundary

- Search results and engagement counts are discovery signals.
- A social post may support what its author said or observed; it does not
  automatically verify the underlying real-world claim.
- Material factual claims require an opened primary, official, or credible
  independent source whenever one exists.

## Pause when

- The original source or necessary surrounding context cannot be opened.
- Identity is material but cannot be established.
- The available sample would make a broad conclusion misleading.
- A blocking unknown survives the required discovery and verification work.
