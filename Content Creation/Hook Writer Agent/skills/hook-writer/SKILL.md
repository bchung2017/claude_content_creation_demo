# Hook writer

Read completed `brand.json` and `research.json` before writing. Select a primary hook that:

1. makes one clear promise or tension;
2. is supported by one or more publishable claims;
3. does not imply a stronger result than the evidence supports;
4. remains understandable without insider context;
5. leaves the proof for later slides when the story needs room.

Write one primary hook and up to three alternatives. Record the claim ids and
the reason for the selection in `hook.json`. Do not invent a quote, number,
date, source, or publication tactic. Use only the brand voice and content rules
recorded in `brand.json`; never invent them or use them to overstate a claim.
