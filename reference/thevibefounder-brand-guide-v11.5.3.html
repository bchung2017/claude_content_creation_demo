01 / Brand idea

The work is the proof.

The Vibe Founder is built around visible progress. The brand should feel like someone opened the workshop while the work was still happening: direct, useful, and specific enough to verify.

01 / Build live Let people see the process.

Use real sessions, real screens, real decisions, and real constraints. The work should not feel reconstructed after the fact.

02 / Name the state Say what is happening now.

Live, replay, shipped, blocked, testing, or archived. Status language turns activity into something people can follow.

03 / Show the receipt Make the evidence visible.

A result, artifact, timestamp, commit, invoice, or decision is more persuasive than an abstract promise.

02 / Identity

Full name when there is room. V-mark when there is not.

The wordmark carries recognition. The V-mark carries speed. They are related, but they do different jobs.

Optical contrast modes

06 / Caption system

Caption the idea. Keep the proof.

The reference video wins by separating speech, context, demonstration, and personality. TVF uses the same hierarchy with its own type system: Inter speaks, Geist Mono proves, DM Serif promotes, and italic adds one human aside.

01 / Speech Inter speaks.

The readable layer. Sentence case, breath-grouped, one or two lines. It carries most of the transcript.

02 / Subtext MONO PROVES.

State, artifact, time, version, or result. It adds context and never repeats the spoken line.

03 / Hero Serif promotes.

One to three words at the hook or payoff. Scarce enough to feel like an event.

04 / Annotation Italic humanizes.

A rare aside, analogue to handwriting. It never carries information the viewer must read.

Long-form / interviews · tutorials · replay

Readable first.
Designed in chapters.

Speech
Inter 600 / 48px at 1080p / 1.12 line-height / two lines / 42 characters per line.
Placement
Lower-middle, above player chrome and the bottom-left brand lockup. 10% horizontal / 12% bottom safe area.
Timing
1–4 seconds per phrase, 0.5 second absolute minimum. Fade + 6px rise in 150–200ms.
Hero
DM Serif 88–112px, one to three words, approximately once per chapter or every 30–45 seconds.
Subtext
Geist Mono 22px, one line, held 3–6 seconds. Use for state and receipts—not decoration.
Short-form / reels · shorts · tiktok

Fast crown.
Earned hero.

Speech
Inter 600 / 64–76px at 1920h / one to four words per frame / two lines maximum.
Placement
Crown at y 12–28%. Shift away from the face. The bottom 22% stays empty for platform UI.
Timing
0.55–1.4 seconds per phrase. 120ms fade/slide. Hero holds 1–1.8 seconds.
Hero
DM Serif 144–196px, one to three words, optionally behind the subject. Never two heroes together.
Rhythm
One dark/cream interstitial or hero reset every 8–12 seconds. Amber is the wipe or rule, not the transcript.
Editorial law / 70–85% displayed

Drop filler, exact stutters, abandoned corrections, and obvious visual echoes. Segment on a natural pause of 250ms or a complete clause. Clear the frame for rhetorical silence longer than 1.5 seconds. Never grade the footage to rescue a weak caption placement.
